Presentation Story

1. Museum zone sensors publish temperature, humidity, light, motion, and battery telemetry via MQTT. 2. The backend updates a lightweight digital twin for each zone. 3. Dashboard visualizes preservation risks, sensor reliability, and recent telemetry. 4. CoAP provides lightweight query and protection actuator control. 5. Desired and reported actuator states stay synchronized in the digital twin.
